What We Offer

Spanish Cuisine,
Your Way

From intimate tapas gatherings to large-scale paella events, we tailor every detail to your vision.

01
Tapas Catering

A curated selection of traditional and modern Spanish tapas, served buffet-style or passed. Croquetas, gambas al ajillo, patatas bravas, and more — crafted with seasonal ingredients and careful technique. Perfect for intimate gatherings and social events.

02
Paella Experience

Live paella cooking at your event — an interactive and memorable centerpiece that brings guests together. Watch the rice bloom in saffron broth, the socarrat form, and the moment of reveal. It becomes the highlight every time.

03
Full-Service Catering

From food to staffing, we deliver seamless catering experiences tailored to your event size, style, and vision. Menu planning, setup, service, and cleanup — so you can be fully present for your guests.

Our Story

Rooted in Tradition,
Driven by Connection

Rooted in Spanish culinary tradition, SF Tapas brings authentic flavors and a passion for hospitality to every event across the Bay Area. From family recipes to carefully sourced ingredients, our approach blends heritage with a thoughtful modern catering experience.

“We believe great food creates connection — and every event deserves that experience.”

Every dish we prepare carries that philosophy. The socarrat at the bottom of the paella, the perfectly seasoned croqueta, the olive oil drizzled over fresh bread — these details are not accidents. They are intention.
